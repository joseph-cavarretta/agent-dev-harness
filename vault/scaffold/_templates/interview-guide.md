## Questions to Ask

### About the work
1. What does a successful first 6 months look like? What's the first problem you'd hand me?
2. Where is the biggest technical pain right now?
3. How does the team decide when something is good enough?

### About focus and priorities
4. How are priorities set, and how often do they change? What's the planning cadence?
5. How is the team shielded from interrupt-driven requests?
6. Would this role own a defined domain, or float across teams?
7. What does on-call look like, and how often does it get paged?

### About culture and stability
8. How would you describe the pace here?
9. How big is the team, and how has it changed over the past year?
10. What does the in-office / remote policy look like in practice? Any focus days?
11. What do people who do well on your team have in common?

## Green Flags / Red Flags to Listen For

**Green**
- Clear team ownership and stable planning cadence
- Concrete metrics and goals for the team
- Quiet, well-run on-call
- Manager protects the team's focus

**Red**
- Vague answers on priorities, team size changes, or scope
- Role absorbs ad-hoc support work
- Every new launch pulls the team off its roadmap
